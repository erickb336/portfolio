A Short Stay in Hell

About this book ↗
My review

I really enjoyed the book. It was a quick read for an afternoon with an interesting premise. I liked the pacing and subplots but wish there was a happier ending. I was left a bit confused on what this hell was trying to teach its guests. My takeaway is that this is something about human nature and searching and hope. This hell had many emergent qualities of life on Earth. I guess humans will always be human no matter their environment. They will love and grieve and play and spend their time solving problems and searching for something. On Earth, like in Hell, we hope that someone or something whether it be God or something else can witness and validate our struggles and efforts.
